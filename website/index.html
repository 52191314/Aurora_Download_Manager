Engineered for Speed & Privacy

Built from the ground up for power users who demand full control over their network downloads.

⚡

Multi-Thread Splitting

Accelerate downloads by dynamically splitting files into up to 32 parallel segments, maximizing bandwidth utilization.

🛡️

Zero Ads & Privacy-First

No popups, no ad trackers, and no Google Play services required. Your download URLs and data remain entirely private on device.

📁

Smart File Categorization

Auto-sort finished downloads into custom categories: Videos, Audio, Archives, Documents, Software, and custom paths.

☁️

WebDAV Cloud Backup

Sync your app preferences, active queues, and download logs seamlessly across your devices using WebDAV protocols.

🌐

Media Link Sniffer

Integrated web browser component detects downloadable media resources, direct video streams, and audio links in 1 tap.

🔋

Smart Queue & Battery Rules

Pause or resume downloads based on Wi-Fi connection, battery saver state, or custom scheduled download time windows.
